Phonetically Grounded Vowel Space Metrics for Evaluating Synthetic Speech During TTS Model Training

Pasindu Udawatta, Jesin James, Ake Nicholas, B.T. Balamurali, C.I. Watson
Interspeech 2026 • Conference of the International Speech Communication Association • Sydney, Australia

Proposed phonetically grounded metrics to objectively evaluate synthetic speech during TTS model training. Demonstrated that the proposed metrics correlate significantly with human accent perception, providing perceptually meaningful, and interpretable complements to conventional loss curves.

TTSVowelViz: A Tool for Visualising Text-to-Speech Model Training via Vowel Spaces

Pasindu Udawatta, Jesin James, B.T. Balamurali, Catherine Watson, Ake Nicholas, Binu Abeysinghe
LREC 2026 • Language Resources and Evaluation Conference • Palma, Spain

Introduced TTSVowelViz, a tool that visualises vowel spaces during text-to-speech model training, bridging linguistic knowledge and AI development. It tracks how vowel sounds are learned over time, revealing linguistic characteristics and improving interpretability beyond traditional loss curves.

Use of Prompt-Based Learning for Code-Mixed and Code-Switched Text Classification

Pasindu Udawatta, Indunil Udayangana, Chathulanka Gamage, Ravi Shekhar, Surangika Ranathunga
World Wide Web • Internet and Web Information Systems • Volume 27, Article Number 63

Explored code-mixed and code-switched text classification using prompt-based learning and introduced a novel approach which combines script-aware modeling with task-specific adapters. Experiments showed that proposed method outperforms standard fine-tuning and prompting techniques.

Can Vowel Space Visualisation Reveal How Text-to-Speech Systems Learn?

Pasindu Udawatta, Jesin James, Catherine Watson, Ake Nicholas, B.T. Balamurali
NZLingSoc 2025 • Conference of the Linguistic Society of New Zealand • Wellington, New Zealand

Proposed using vowel space visualisation to evaluate how well text-to-speech systems learn vowel sounds. Results showed that as the system develops, its vowel production becomes more similar to real speech, aligning with human perception and demonstrating the method’s effectiveness for evaluating speech learning.

TTSVowelViz: A Tool for Visualising Text-to-Speech Model Training Through Vowel Spaces

Pasindu Udawatta, Jesin James, B.T. Balamurali, Catherine Watson, Ake Nicholas, Binu Abeysinghe
ICASSP 2025 - Show & Tell • IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing • Hyderabad, India

TTSVowelViz is an interactive tool that visualises vowel spaces in real-time during text-to-speech model training. By mapping vowel sounds using formant frequencies, it helps to identify which sounds are accurately learned, enabling early detection of issues without costly retraining.
